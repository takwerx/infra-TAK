MFA Device Recovery
A user who lost the phone with their authenticator app cannot sign in and cannot reset it themselves — by design, so a password-reset email can never be an MFA bypass. Find them here and revoke their registered devices; they will be walked through setting up a new authenticator the next time they sign in.
Break-glass — your own admin account
Use the search above for everyone else. These two are for when you have lost the authenticator for the account you administer with. They also work when the Authentik API is unreachable — the search cannot — by falling back to ak shell.
If this console is only reachable behind Authentik SSO, losing the admin authenticator locks you out of this page too. The way back in is an SSH tunnel to the console port, then this button.
TAK clients are not affected. MFA covers web sign-ins only (this console, TAK Portal, Node-RED, NetBird, WebODM). TAK clients authenticate by certificate over LDAP, which is never MFA-gated — a lost phone does not knock anyone off the map.
Verify identity out of band before revoking — a phone call or in-person check, not an email to the address on the account.

About Authentik
Authentik is an open-source Identity Provider supporting SSO, SAML, OAuth2/OIDC, LDAP, and RADIUS.

It provides centralized user authentication and management for all your services — including TAK Portal for TAK Server user/cert management and MediaMTX for stream access and user/group management (with or without TAK Portal).

Deploys: PostgreSQL + Redis + Authentik Server + Worker (4 containers)
Ports: 9090 (HTTP) · 9443 (HTTPS)
Recommended: 2+ CPU cores, 2+ GB RAM
Deployment Target ▾
Password is never stored. It is used only for one-time ssh-copy-id.

Identity Bridge Agency AD/Entra roster → auto agency + TAK template
Agencies manage TAK users from their own Active Directory / Entra: a SCIM feed pushes their roster into Authentik ahead of first login, and the console auto-assigns agency + TAK Portal template from the mapping table below. The bridge only ever manages channels it granted itself — anything you set by hand is never touched, and pinned users are skipped entirely. When AD moves someone to a different role group, they move to that template's channels and lose the old ones. Runs automatically every 5 minutes.
Creates (or reuses) the agency's SCIM source in Authentik and shows the two values their IT team pastes into Entra provisioning.
