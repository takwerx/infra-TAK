• Let's Encrypt only checks the standard ports 80/443. Use a Custom Certificate, or have your router/firewall forward public 80 → the HTTP port (and 443 → the HTTPS port). Otherwise certificates will not issue or renew.
• Modules that save their own copy of a URL (TAK Portal's public URL, NetBird, Remote Assist, ATLAS, CloudTAK) keep the old one until you update or redeploy them. Setting the ports before deploying other modules avoids this.
• Allow the new ports in any cloud security group or router in front of this server.

Set these only if your PKI policy names a different CA — Sectigo, DigiCert, Google Trust Services, or an internal ACME server. Public CAs other than Let's Encrypt issue you an External Account Binding key pair that ties ACME to your paid account.

Changing the CA reissues every certificate on this box within about a minute — it is not deferred until renewal.
